One Night

Tonight you see Wren again — the almost that never quite happened, the one you never let yourself have. Four women live in this game, and by sunrise it will tell you which one is you — not by asking, but by watching how you love: how you fall, how you guard, and whether you let someone all the way in.

There's no “right” one to aim for. The scenes are ordinary on purpose and the answers don't line up with anyone the way you'd guess. Just move through the night as you would.
