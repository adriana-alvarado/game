One Night

Four women live in this game, and by sunrise it will tell you which one is you. You won't answer a quiz — you'll live an evening, from getting ready to the walk home, and the small choices you make along the way will quietly give you away.

There's no “right” one to aim for. The scenes are ordinary on purpose and the answers don't line up with anyone the way you'd guess. Just move through the night as you would.
