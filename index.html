The Read

There are four of them in here, and this game will tell you which one you are. But it won't ask you who you'd date or hand you a line to recognize — it measures the machinery underneath: how you decide, how you fight, what your jokes are really doing, and what it takes to change your mind.

No favorites. The situations are deliberately ordinary and the answers don't line up with anyone the way you'd guess — you can't aim for a character. The only way to get an honest read is to answer as you actually are.
